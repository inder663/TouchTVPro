Overview

Touch TV Pro is designed to help users control their television through their mobile device seamlessly. The application offers various functionalities such as customizable buttons, gesture binding, and screen casting to enhance the user experience.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information we may collect when you use our app.

  • No personal data is collected from users.
  • We do not use analytics tools.
  • In-app purchases are available for enhanced features.

Information We Collect

Touch TV Pro does not collect any personal information from its users. We respect your privacy and are committed to ensuring that your information remains secure.

How We Use Data

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no data to use for any purpose. All features within the app function without needing to gather personal information.

Sharing

We do not share any user data with third parties. Your information remains confidential and secure within the app.

Analytics

Touch TV Pro does not employ any analytics services. We do not track user activity, ensuring a completely private user experience.

Cookies/Tracking

The app does not use cookies or other tracking technologies to collect user data.

Data Retention

As we do not collect any personal data, there is no retention policy for user information. All app interactions occur in real-time and do not require data storage.

Security

Children's Privacy

Touch TV Pro does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware of any information collected from children, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.
